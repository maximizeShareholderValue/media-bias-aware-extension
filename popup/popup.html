    How these numbers are computed

    Bias Index = (sum of matched rules' confidence weights ÷ words analyzed) × 1000, capped at 100. It only reflects the rule-based phrase matches you can see highlighted in the article - it is not a machine-learned or opaque score.

    Reliability % is a fixed mapping from the outlet's factual-reliability tier (Very High=95, High=80, Mostly Factual=65, Mixed=45, Low=20), sourced from the bundled outlet dataset.

    Every highlighted phrase in the article traces to a specific named rule ID - hover or focus any highlight to see it.
