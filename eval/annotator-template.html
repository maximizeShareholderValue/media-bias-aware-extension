Article annotation (Phase 1)

Rules

  • Judge the wording only: not the politics, not whether it is true.
  • Mark only clear cases. Unsure? Leave it out.
  • Select only the words that carry the technique, not the whole sentence.
  • Skip grey text: it is quoted speech.
  • One phrase, two techniques? Mark it twice.
  • Work alone.

Steps

  1. Here: mark all 30 articles and tick "finished" on each. Then click Export Phase 1 CSV and send the file.
  2. Then click Phase 2 (top bar): mark each of the 19 phrases Valid or Not Valid, then click Export Phase 2 CSV and send the file. Opening Phase 2 locks Step 1.

Use the same browser every time; your work saves automatically. Export Phase 1 CSV at the end of every sitting as a backup.
